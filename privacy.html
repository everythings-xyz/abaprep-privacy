ABAPrep

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026

ABAPrep is a study app for SAP ABAP certification and interview preparation. It asks for an email address so your Pro purchase can be restored on a new device, and nothing more. Your study progress never leaves your phone.

What we collect

Data Why
Email address To create your account and sign you in. Required so a Pro purchase can be restored if you change or reset your device.
Purchase status A single record of whether you own the Pro unlock. Payment itself is handled by Google Play — we never see or store card details.
Question reports Only if you choose to report a question. Stores which question and what you wrote, so the content can be corrected.

What stays on your device

Your progress, streaks, weak-spot list, daily-challenge status and quiz history are stored locally on your phone and are not transmitted to us. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers. ABAPrep shows no ads.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos, files or device storage.
  • No payment card details.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with third parties for their own purposes.

Who processes your data

ABAPrep uses a small number of service providers to operate:

  • Supabase — hosts the account system and the question database.
  • RevenueCat — verifies purchase receipts so your Pro unlock is recognised across devices.
  • Google Play — processes payment and distributes the app, under Google's own privacy policy.

These providers process data on our behalf. They are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

How long we keep it

Your account and purchase record are kept while your account exists. If you delete your account, they are removed. Question reports are kept in anonymised form after review so content corrections remain traceable.

Deleting your account

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing everythings.xyz@gmail.com from the address you registered with. Requests are actioned within 30 days.

Deleting your account does not refund purchases. Refunds are handled by Google Play.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete the personal data we hold about you, and to object to its processing. Email everythings.xyz@gmail.com to exercise any of these. Because we hold only your email address and a purchase flag, most requests are quick to fulfil.

Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored on Supabase's managed infrastructure. Access to your account data is restricted by database-level security policies so that one user cannot read another's records. No system is perfectly secure, but the amount of personal data held is deliberately minimal.

Children

ABAPrep is aimed at working developers and students preparing for professional SAP certification. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and it will be removed.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top will be updated and, where the change affects how your data is used, you will be notified in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: everythings.xyz@gmail.com
